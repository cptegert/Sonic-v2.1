Speed learning where the robot slows down or speeds up – learned per robot and per track

Mode:
After every regular lap, per part of the track: trouble there (closer than 15 cm to a wall, reversing, stuck) → 10 % slower there, and it starts braking one part earlier. Getting closer to the wall than usual (sliding wider, dirty wheels) → 3 % slower. Clean → back up. Speed calibration find the best speed for this track

The laptop drives the robot by itself at several speeds, one run per speed (repeated), and counts the laps per minute. Crashes and reversing cost time, so the best score = fastest reliable speed.
  1. Robot switched on, laptop connected to it (green “Wi-Fi live” at the top)
  2. Main switch OFF in the app / button not pressed (robot not driving)
  3. Manual mode OFF
  4. Phone app disconnected – it would overwrite the speed
Each run: 5 s countdown → drives → stops by itself (also if the Wi-Fi drops) → short break → next speed, from wherever it stopped. “Pause after this run” = e.g. battery swap, then Continue. Your speed is put back at the end; “Apply” saves the best one.

The robot writes every drive into its own memory: laps with times and trouble, the speed map, your marks, wrong-way and grip notes – also in a competition when no laptop is connected (about a whole day fits). Afterwards: connect to the robot's Wi-Fi, robot stopped, press Read.
