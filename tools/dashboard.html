Click to add corner points in driving order (any start), drag to move, double-click a point to delete it. The line closes by itself and corners are rounded. Hill: press it, then click the first and the last point of the hill.
Pieces in driving order, separated by spaces: S2 = straight 2 units, L90 / R45 = left / right turn in degrees (L90r2 = wider, radius 2), H1 = hill 1 unit long. Example: S3 L90 S1 H1 L90 S3 L180 R90 L90. All turns together should add up to 360° (left minus right).
